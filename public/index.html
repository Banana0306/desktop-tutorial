About Us

45 Years of Trust in the Motorcycle Industry

We began in 1981 as a Bridgestone motorcycle parts distributor and have grown into one of Taiwan's most trusted wholesale distributors of premium tire and braking system brands for international markets.

Our deep relationships with global manufacturers — built over decades — allow us to offer competitive pricing, reliable logistics, and expert product knowledge that our partners rely on.

  • Authorized wholesale distributor for 6+ internationally recognized tire brands
  • Importer of Brembo precision braking systems
  • OEM tire manufacturing experience with Taiwanese factories
  • 45+ years of industry relationships and technical expertise
