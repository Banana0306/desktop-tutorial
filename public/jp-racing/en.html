About Us

Jeou Pao Enterprise Co., Ltd. (JP Racing) has been deeply rooted in motorcycle parts wholesale for many years. We are the authorized distributor for world-renowned brands including Bridgestone, Dunlop, Pirelli, and IRC, and we own our proprietary OEM brands — HOVR motor oil and JP Racing brake systems — committed to being your most trusted wholesale partner.
